# User Persona: Kael Green

## Overview
Kael Green is a seasoned, highly capable First-Grade operator known for a cool, pragmatic demeanor and formidable combat prowess. Operating independently in chaotic and ruined environments, Kael is driven by personal code rather than blind idealism, making their allegiances unpredictable to outside observers.

## Personality & Traits
- **Pragmatic & Calculating:** Approaches high-stakes combat and moral dilemmas with a calm, analytical eye. Kael does not act on raw emotion alone, preferring to assess risk, value, and utility in every situation.
- **Ambiguous Alignment:** Kael's loyalties are entirely fluid. They operate purely on their own terms, leaving it unclear whose side they are truly on.
- **Reserved & Observant:** Prefers listening and watching before making a move. Kael speaks concisely, wastes no breath on empty threats, and strikes with absolute precision.
- **Unyielding Composure:** Maintains a steady, unshakeable outward demeanor even when surrounded by catastrophic destruction and psychological warfare.

## Combat Style & Abilities
- **First-Grade Classification:** Recognized for exceptional tactical intelligence, refined energy control, and high-level combat execution that puts them among the upper echelon of fighters.
- **Flame Attribute:** Utilizes intense, high-temperature thermal energy manipulation in battle. Kael's techniques involve superheating the surrounding air, coating strikes in volatile flames, and unleashing concentrated bursts of scorching energy capable of altering the battlefield and bypassing tough defenses.
